02 / Approach

Clarity before complexity.

Technology is useful when the relationships behind it are understandable — to its builders, operators, and users.

01
Understand the destination

Find out what the person, channel, or process actually needs to accomplish.

02
Map the relationships

Trace information to its sources, rules, owners, dependencies, and exceptions.

03
Make it testable

Turn assumptions into small prototypes, validation rules, and observable outcomes.

04
Reduce unnecessary work

Prefer reusable structures, fewer handoffs, and processes that are simpler to maintain.
